About

Current Development

DecScale 32-bit

DecScale is a specialized 32-bit hardware IP combining dedicated decimal scaling and arithmetic functions in a single digital architecture.

The design is being developed and evaluated through digital logic simulation, FPGA prototyping, synthesis, physical implementation, and ASIC-oriented analysis.
